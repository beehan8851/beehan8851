# Companion sounds

Every recording behind the companions' voices (`app/src/main/res/raw/pet_*.ogg`), from
[Freesound](https://freesound.org). All are **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication): free for any use,
commercial included, with no attribution required; each licence was checked on the sound's own page
on 2026-10-08. They are credited here anyway, so where every sound came from is never a question.

The files were cut, filtered (high-pass 70 Hz), levelled to match the cat's sounds (alarm about −13 LUFS,
calm loop about −27 LUFS, taps peaking at −4.3 dBFS) and encoded as mono Vorbis. The cat's own sounds
(`meow`, `mrrp1–3`, `purr`) came with the iOS app; their source is not recorded in either project.
